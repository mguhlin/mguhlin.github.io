# Image provenance

## Original generated image

`assets/images/workspace.webp` was generated with the built-in image-generation
tool on September 29, 2026, then resized and encoded as WebP for the site. It is an
illustrative scene, not a photograph of Miguel's actual workspace. Its alt text
and the site footer identify the use of generated imagery.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial hero photograph for Miguel
> Guhlin's personal directory of creative browser tools, learning resources, and
> games. Create a wide landscape photograph, 3:2 composition, of a real independent
> educator-maker's sunlit workspace: a closed navy blue cloth notebook with a pencil
> across it, an open cream sketchbook with a few subtle hand-drawn geometric diagrams
> (no legible words), a modern laptop partially visible at the far right displaying
> an abstract muted blue creative canvas (no text or logos), a small ceramic coffee
> cup, and natural oak desk grain. Through a softly blurred window behind the desk,
> a leafy Texas garden in morning light. Quiet curiosity, thoughtful craftsmanship,
> inviting and lived-in, documentary editorial photography, natural irregularity
> and believable materials. Camera slightly above desk height at a three-quarter
> angle, objects arranged with breathing room, avoid overfilled flat lay. Muted navy,
> slate blue, warm wood, soft off-white. Realistic light and shadow, no illustration,
> no artificial neon, no text, no watermark, no people.
