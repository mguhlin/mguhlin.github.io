ABOUT DEVELOPER

Meet Miguel Guhlin.

Postbox is created by Miguel Guhlin, the developer behind SplatWorks and a collection of practical tools for learning, creating, and everyday work.

Postbox gives exported email a useful home on your own computer: find a message, preview an attachment, organize an archive, and keep the files you need close at hand.
